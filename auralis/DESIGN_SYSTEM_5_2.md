# 5.2 Interaction and Visual Design Rules

Keep the Studio 4 editorial identity: Bodoni Moda + DM Sans + IBM Plex Mono; paper #F1EEE5, charcoal #27332D and olive #858B67. Avoid neon, purple-blue gradients, useless cards and fake badges. Real interview elements and clear status labels take visual priority.

The interview realism controls live in the existing Preparation step, not in a new dashboard route. The Interview Room displays persona and track as a small readable stamp. Question time is readable but never creates a punitive lockout. The overall interview clock and the per-question clock are visually distinct. Voice feedback is text-based with explicit limitations, is absent for typed-only answers, and never alters Skill DNA scoring.

Responsive constraints: no horizontal overflow from 360 to 1440 px; keyboard-usable selects/buttons; no color-only alerts; print hides room controls and preserves report evidence.
