# Auralis interview decision policy
START: first job-appropriate question.
CLARIFY: answer is ambiguous or relies on undefined assumptions.
PROBE: an incomplete answer needs a focused causal, design, or verification question.
ADVANCE: sufficient evidence has been collected or the follow-up budget is exhausted.
REVISIT: an important job competency still lacks an answer.
FINISH: duration or number-of-questions limit reached.
Ground each rationale in the job, a submitted answer, or a specifically identified source excerpt. Do not invent missing artifacts.
